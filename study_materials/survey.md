## Page 1 — Welcome

**Welcome to the Study**

Thank you for participating in this research study.

**What this study is about**

We are investigating how well current voice-assisted programming tools support real coding work.
Over the course of this study, you will use one such tool to complete a series of small
programming tasks entirely by voice — without touching the keyboard to type code or the mouse to
navigate files.

**What you will do**

This survey has three parts:

1. **Right now (this part):** A few questions about your background and programming experience,
   followed by your consent to participate. This takes about 5 minutes.
2. **The coding session:** After this survey section, you will be directed to a browser-based
   virtual desktop environment. A voice coding tool will already be set up and ready to use. Your
   task is to complete as many of the provided coding exercises as you can within 10 minutes,
   using only your voice. Please read the task instructions carefully when you arrive — they are
   displayed inside the environment.
3. **After the session:** You will be brought back to this survey to answer a short set of
   questions about your experience. You will receive your Prolific completion code at the very
   end.

**Before you continue, please make sure:**
- You are on a desktop or laptop (not a phone or tablet)
- Your microphone is connected and working
- You have a stable internet connection

If anything is unclear at any point, you can contact the researchers [contact — redacted].

*(Consent form and pre-experiment questions follow on subsequent pages)*

Fields: **STUDY ID**, **SESSION ID** (pre-filled; participants are told not to alter them).

## Page 3 — Session entry

You are now ready to begin the hands-free coding session.

**Before you click the button below, please read carefully:**
- The session will open in a new browser tab. Do not close this SurveyXact tab — you will need
  to return to it when you are done.
- When the environment loads, a short welcome message will appear with instructions for your
  assigned tool. Read it, then click OK to begin.
- Complete as many coding tasks as you can within 10 minutes. The tasks are listed in the task
  page inside the session.
- You may use the keyboard and mouse only to activate or pause the voice tool (e.g. a
  push-to-talk key). Do not type code or use the mouse to navigate files.
- When you are finished — or when 10 minutes have passed — return to this survey, and continue
  finishing the survey.

**If you experience a technical problem** (the environment does not load, your microphone is not
detected, the tool crashes): close the tab, return here, and contact the researchers
[contact — redacted] before proceeding.
