## **Purpose of the Research**

This research study explores how people use voice-assisted programming tools to complete coding
tasks without relying on keyboard and mouse input. The goal is to understand usability challenges
and measure productivity and accessibility when using such tools. The study involves a hands-on
session with a voice coding tool.

## **Compensation for Participation**

You will receive compensation through Prolific, the platform we/as well as you use for this
study. You will receive a competitive rate for participating in this study. We thank you for that.

## **Procedures and Protocol**

**Hands-on Session (approx. 20–30 mins):** After reading this information and completing the
consent form below, you will be asked to use a voice-assisted coding tool inside a browser-based
virtual desktop environment (Kasm Workspaces). You are required to use the tool for at least
**10 minutes**. After completing the session, you will fill out a short exit survey.

During the session, your **keystroke and mouse events** will be logged automatically (every
keypress, mouse movement, and click). This data is used to measure how much you rely on
traditional input methods versus voice. We also log session start time, end time, and number of
completed workflows.

If the tool is not working at all (no response to your voice whatsoever), please contact the
researchers rather than sitting idle. We ask that you make a genuine effort to use the tool for the
full 10 minutes.
